Tears GPS

A GPS setter based on the LSPosed framework
v{{VERSION}} libxposed API 102 Android 8.1+ GPL-3.0

What it does

Tears GPS replaces your device location with a point you choose on the map. Apps see the simulated position as a normal GPS fix, without Android's "mock location" setting.

It can also move that point: with a floating joystick, along routes you record, or along a real road route from A to B by car, bike or on foot, with a realistic speed.

Requirements

  • A rooted device (Magisk, KernelSU, SukiSU…) with Zygisk.
  • LSPosed with libxposed API 102 support (LSPosed 2.x).
  • Android 8.1 or newer.

Quick start

  1. In LSPosed, enable Tears GPS and tick System Framework in its scope.
  2. Reboot.
  3. Open Tears GPS: no "module not active" message means it is connected to LSPosed.
  4. Tap the map to choose a point, then press ▶. Press ■ to stop.
Enable only one location module of this kind (e.g. GPS Setter) in LSPosed at a time.

System-wide or per app

Settings → Hook system location

  • On — every app gets the simulated location (hooked inside the Android system).
  • Off — only the apps you tick in the LSPosed scope are affected.

The switch works live, no reboot needed. A reboot is only needed after changing the System Framework entry in the LSPosed scope.

Settings

General

  • Dark theme — light, dark or follow the system.
  • Map — OpenStreetMap styles: Liberty (detailed streets), Bright, Positron (light) or Dark.
  • Check for updates — looks for a new Tears GPS release on GitHub when the app starts.
  • Use joystick — tap to show or hide the floating joystick.
  • Joystick speed — speed at full push: walk 5, run 11, bike 20, car 50 km/h.

Xposed

  • Hook system location — on: every app gets the simulated location; off: only apps ticked in the LSPosed scope. Applies immediately.
  • Accuracy — the accuracy (in metres) reported with the simulated fix. A real phone outdoors reports roughly 3–15 m.
  • Random location — adds a small random offset (up to a few tens of metres) around the point on every update, so the position looks like normal GPS jitter instead of a perfectly fixed point.

The map

  • Tap — moves the point and shows its coordinates (with Copy).
  • Long-press — Copy, Route from here (A) or Route to here (B), without moving the point.
  • Search — address, place or coordinates like 42.69, 23.32.
  • ◎ button — goes to the simulated point while spoofing is on, to your real location when it is off.
  • ☆ — save the point as a favorite.

Joystick

Settings → Use joystick shows a floating joystick over other apps (it needs the "display over other apps" permission and a started point).

  • Push gently to walk slowly, fully for full speed.
  • Joystick speed: walk 5, run 11, bike 20 or car 50 km/h.
  • Its notification has Record and Stop.

Routes

Menu → Routes

  • Record — start the joystick, tap Record in its notification, move around, then Save. Pauses are kept.
  • New route A → B — From (empty = current point) and To (address, place or coordinates like 42.87, 25.32). Tip: long-press the map to fill them in.
  • Car — real road speeds: every stretch is driven at the speed its road type and limit allow, so 30–50 km/h in town, faster on main roads and motorways, slowing down in turns. A 2 h trip takes 2 h.
  • Bike / Foot — cycling and walking speeds on bike paths, footways and streets.
  • Play — once, with the route's own timing (recorded pauses included).
  • Play in a loop — starts again from the beginning at the end; meant for laps (a park, a stadium).
  • Round trip — A → B, stays at B for 1, 3, 5, 10, 20 or 30 min or 1, 2, 4, 6, 12 or 24 h, then comes back B → A and stops. The way back is a separately calculated real route (one-way streets differ).
  • Rename / Delete — manage saved routes. Stop playback appears while a route is playing.

The notification shows the progress (→ B, time left at the destination, ← A) and a Stop button; tap it to open the map at the current position. ■ in the app stops spoofing and the route together; Stop in the notification ends only the route and keeps the point where it is.

Keeping it running

  • Routes keep moving with the screen off.
  • While a route or the joystick moves the point, apps also get a matching speed and direction (a navigation app shows its arrow). Standing still or jumping to a new point reports no movement.
  • If the system kills the app, routes and the joystick come back by themselves and continue from where they should be now.
  • After a Force stop, Android does not allow any restart until you open the app again; the route then continues from the right place.
On Xiaomi / HyperOS: enable Autostart, set battery saver to No restrictions and lock Tears GPS in the recent apps screen.

Privacy and network

  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads.
  • Creating an A → B route sends the start and end points to the OSRM routing server of FOSSGIS (routing.openstreetmap.de).
  • Searching a place may send the text to OpenStreetMap Nominatim when the phone's own geocoder finds nothing.
  • Saved routes stay on the device. Playing them needs no internet.
Troubleshooting

Module log (expect hooking system_server and LocationProviderManager: 2 hook(s)):

su -c '/system/bin/grep -ihE "tearsgps" /data/adb/lspd/log/modules_*.log | /system/bin/tail -20'

Last app crash:

su -c '/system/bin/logcat -d | /system/bin/grep -A 20 "FATAL EXCEPTION" | /system/bin/tail -40'
  • Location not changed — System Framework ticked in the scope? Rebooted after enabling? Point started (■ visible)?
  • "Module not active" — the module is disabled in LSPosed or LSPosed does not support API 102.
  • Route failed — no internet, or the routing server is busy; try again.
How it was made

Tears GPS revives GPS Setter, abandoned since March 2025 on the legacy Xposed API. It was ported to the modern libxposed API 102, rebuilt on a current toolchain and extended with new features. Built and tested on a Poco F6 Pro with HyperOS.

Main changes

  • Legacy Xposed API 93 → libxposed API 102; settings through LSPosed Remote Preferences instead of deprecated world-readable files.
  • System-wide live spoofing on Android 14+ (every location report in the system is rewritten, not only the last known location).
  • Live settings without reboot; raw GNSS data blocked while spoofing.
  • New: working joystick, route recording and playback, road routes A → B, round trips, map point picking, better search, screen-off and kill-proof playback.
  • Fixed crashes and bugs from the original (favorites, search, joystick and more).
  • Realistic motion: speed and direction are worked out from how the point moves, so navigation apps show a heading arrow and follow routes and the joystick smoothly.

Languages and tools: Kotlin, Gradle (Groovy DSL), XML, Bash.
